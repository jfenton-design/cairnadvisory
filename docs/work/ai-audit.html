Work Service overview

The AI Audit — a clear picture of where AI belongs in your business.

Most businesses don't lack ideas for AI. They lack clarity on what to prioritize, what to ignore, and what will actually work in their environment. The AI Audit cuts through the noise and produces decisions — not a slide deck of possibilities.

Engagement Fixed-fee audit
Timeline 1–2 weeks
Format Remote or on-site
Investment $2,500 flat
How it works

Three steps. One useful answer.

A · Discovery

Understanding how your business actually operates.

  • One leadership interview with the primary stakeholder (up to 60 min)
  • Two to three team or operator interviews (~30 min each)
  • Review of relevant documents, systems, and tools you provide
  • Observation of key workflows, as agreed upon
B · Analysis

Where AI fits — and where it doesn't.

  • Identification of gaps between current state and AI-ready operations
  • Mapping of automation and AI opportunities across the business
  • Assessment of overall AI readiness
C · Deliverables

A written AI Audit & Roadmap report.

  • A summary of the current state of operations
  • 3–5 prioritized opportunities, ranked by impact and feasibility
  • High-level recommendations with rough cost and effort estimates
  • Suggested next steps and a proposed timeline
Timeline & delivery

Delivered within 1–2 weeks of signed kickoff.

The final deliverable is a written AI Audit & Roadmap report, accompanied by a 60-minute walkthrough meeting to review findings and answer questions.

Implementation & execution support available as a separate engagement.

The point

The AI Audit isn't about "getting ready for AI." It's about making disciplined decisions on where it belongs in your business — and where it doesn't. The real risk right now isn't missing AI. It's wasting time on the wrong version of it.
